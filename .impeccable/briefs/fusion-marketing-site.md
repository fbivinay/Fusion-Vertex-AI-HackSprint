# Fusion marketing site

- **Scope / mode:** Public marketing frontend on Overview (`/`), Solutions (`/solutions`), and Pricing (`/pricing`); Persuade.
- **Audience:** Consultants and delivery leads.
- **Visitor job:** Understand how Fusion turns fragmented project information into traceable requirements, conflicts for human review, and a structured BRD.
- **Primary action:** Explore Fusion; workspace sign-in is a later phase.
- **Proof / content:** User-provided Overview, Solutions, and Pricing screenshots. Plan names, pricing, limits, feature language, and workflow are drawn from those references and product truth; no testimonials or benchmarks.
- **Constraints:** Code-first per the user's earlier selection. Keep the 0–100 violet loader for 3–4 seconds. No old E-commerce content or API-backed workspace data in these public pages. Do not change backend services or stored data.
- **Chosen direction:** User-pinned bright white and pale lavender world with violet accents, a faceted cube mark, split hero with a coded product preview, pastel capability surfaces, and compact pricing comparison. The visual references supersede the generated direction assignment.
- **Memorable moment:** A living, crisp workspace preview that shows documents becoming evidence-linked requirements and a review-ready brief.
- **Seed provenance:** `impeccable concept-seed --scope direction --mode persuade`, key `9a89b875`; the user's explicit screenshot direction is authoritative.
