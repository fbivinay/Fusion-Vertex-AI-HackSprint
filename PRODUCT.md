# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Consultants and delivery leads working with a client or cross-functional team to reconcile fragmented business context and prepare requirements for review.

## Product Purpose

Fusion Vertex AI turns business documents, conversations, screenshots, and data into structured, explainable business requirements and a traceable BRD. Success means teams can follow each requirement to its source, identify contradictions, and make the decisions that remain theirs.

## Positioning

Fusion analyzes multimodal project sources together, then connects extracted requirements and conflicts to supporting evidence and recorded human decisions. It preserves source traceability through BRD generation.

## Operating Context

The workspace supports the flow from project setup and source upload through analysis, requirements, evidence, conflict review, decisions, project-grounded questions, and BRD generation. Consultants and delivery leads use it to prepare and review requirements with stakeholders.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the existing backend, APIs, AI features, source upload and analysis, evidence links, conflict review, decision log, Ask Fusion, and BRD generation.
- Keep human decisions explicit; Fusion presents source differences and their impact but does not decide business intent.
- Retain the existing Gemini analysis path and API contracts.
- Treat extracted requirements, evidence, conflicts, and decisions as operational records that must remain understandable and traceable.
- The user requests a fast, responsive web workspace with restrained layered transparency, readable typography, accessible interactions, purposeful motion, and support for desktop and mobile.
- Avoid invented product claims or fabricated evidence.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Fusion Vertex AI.
- The public marketing site follows the user's white, pale blue-lavender, and violet reference screens, with a faceted Fusion mark and crisp sans-serif typography.
- Keep the landing page, Solutions, and Pricing visually consistent; retain the requested 0–100 entry loader.
- Vercel, Linear, and Stripe are quality references for clarity and interaction polish only; their branding and layouts are not to be copied.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing web implementation in `frontend/src/app/` with working product workflows and API integration.
- Existing API-backed data for projects, sources, requirements, evidence, conflicts, decisions, assistant answers, and BRDs.
- No customer testimonials, external benchmarks, or marketing claims were provided; do not invent them.

## Product Principles

- Keep every requirement explainable through its source evidence.
- Make conflicts and open decisions visible to the people responsible for them.
- Keep human judgment explicit and recorded.
- Preserve the full path from source material to the generated BRD.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The web workspace must remain keyboard-accessible, readable at comfortable sizes, usable on narrow screens, and respectful of reduced-motion preferences.
